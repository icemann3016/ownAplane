# Requests to airfields

Airfields on ownAplane take your arrivals and departures online, with the services you need:
landing, handling, parking or a hangar, fuel, customs and anything else. Open the airfield's page
(**Airports** at the bottom of every page) and press **Request to arrive or depart**. You need a
pilot account.

## From a booking

On an accepted or requested booking, **Airfields on this trip** lists the airfields of your route
that are on ownAplane. **Send a request to …** opens the form filled in from the booking: the
aircraft, the departure or arrival time and the other airfield. Check the times.

## The request

- **Aircraft:** registration, ICAO type and **MTOM**. Prices that depend on weight (landing,
  handling, parking) use the band your aircraft fits in; without the MTOM you see the lightest
  band's price.
- **Arrival and departure:** tick **I'm arriving**, **I'm departing** or both, with the time in
  **UTC** and the airfield you come from or go to. People on board and the purpose.
- **Services:** tick what you need from the airfield's own list, with the quantity where it
  matters (litres of fuel, passengers, nights in the hangar; nights are filled in from your
  times). Landing charges come with an arrival. Items "on request" have no price yet.
- **Other:** anything not on the list, in your own words.

The **rough total** adds up the priced items at the airfield's prices. It's a guide: you pay the
airfield directly, as today, never through ownAplane.

## What happens next

- If the airfield **requires PPR**, your request waits for its answer: **approved** (often with a
  PPR number and maybe conditions, such as a later arrival time) or **declined** with a reason.
- If it **doesn't require PPR**, the request is **confirmed** at once and the airfield sees what
  you need. It can still decline if something can't be provided.

You're notified of every answer (in the app and by email, following your booking notification
settings). **Airfield requests** in your account menu lists all your requests; open one to see the
answer or **cancel** it.

Warnings on a request (outside the opening hours, a closed day, less notice than the airfield asks
for, or crossing a Schengen or EU customs border without asking for customs) are reminders, never
a block.

**Important:** a request on ownAplane is the airfield's administrative permission only. It never
replaces an ATC clearance, a flight plan or the official customs and border notifications.
