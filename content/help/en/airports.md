# Running an airfield

If you run an airfield, you can bring it to ownAplane so pilots send you PPR, parking, hangar and
service requests online instead of by email or phone. An airfield is run by a **team**: managers
and staff. Open **Airports** in your account menu.

## Claim your airfield

1. **Airports → Claim an airfield.** Pick the airfield from the list and give your position (for
   example "Airport manager").
2. Show that you represent it, in one of two ways:
   - **A document:** a letter from the airfield's management, an operator certificate or a similar
     document with your name and the airfield. Only you and our admins see it.
   - **Your airport email:** an address on the airfield's own domain, such as
     `ops@your-airport.com` (free email providers don't count). We send it a 6-digit code; enter it
     under **Your claims** within 30 minutes. **Send a new code** if it didn't arrive.
3. An admin checks the claim, usually within two working days, and you get an email. Once it's
   verified, the airfield is on ownAplane and you are its first **manager**.

You can withdraw an open claim. If it isn't verified, the reason is shown and you can claim again
with other proof. If the airfield already has a team, ask one of its managers to invite you.

## Your team

Open the airfield under **Airports → Your airfields**.

- **Managers** invite people, change roles and remove people, and keep the airfield's page.
  **Staff** see everything and will handle requests.
- **Invite someone:** enter their email and role. They get a link that works for **14 days**, only
  for that address: they log in (or sign up) with it and press **Join the team**. The link is also
  shown to you once, so you can pass it on if the email doesn't arrive. Cancel open invitations at
  any time.
- A team always keeps at least one manager: make someone else a manager before you step down or
  leave.

## The airfield's page

Once the airfield is verified it gets a public page at `ownaplane.eu/airports/` + its code, which
anyone can open, even without an account, and which search engines can find. All verified
airfields are listed under **Airports** at the bottom of every page. Managers keep it up to date
from the tabs on the airfield (staff can look, not change):

- **Profile:** a few lines about the airfield, whether pilots need **PPR** and how many hours'
  notice, services (parking, hangar, handling, customs…), fuels, phone, email and website, and the
  currency of your fees.
- **Opening hours:** tick the days you're open and enter the times in the airfield's **local
  time**, as you know them; **Use Monday's hours for every day** copies Monday to the others. Pilots
  see them in **UTC** for each of the next 7 days, with summer and winter time taken into account,
  so you never convert them yourself. Add a note (for example "Outside these hours on request") and
  **closures** for days the airfield is closed, with a reason pilots see.
- **Runways & radio:** runways (designator, length, width, surface, lights) and frequencies (type,
  MHz, callsign). Airfields start with the runways and frequencies from the public OurAirports
  data; check them and remove or add what's needed.
- **Fees:** landing, parking, hangar and other fees, optionally up to a maximum take-off mass (MTOM),
  per landing, night, day, hour or once. They're shown to pilots only: they pay you as today, not
  through ownAplane.

The page always reminds pilots to check the AIP and NOTAMs: it's information from your team, never
an official source.

## What comes next

Pilots' requests: PPR, parking or hangar, fuel and other services, and customs, with a daily list
for the airfield. A PPR on ownAplane will be the operator's administrative permission only: it
never replaces ATC clearance, a flight plan or official customs and border procedures.
