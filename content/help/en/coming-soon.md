# Coming soon

What we're building next, roughly in this order.

- Airports: pilots' requests to arrive or depart with the services they need from the airfield's
  list, and the airfield's board of arrivals and departures by day, week or month (see
  [Running an airfield](/help/airports)).
- Real card payments for plans and AI credits (today they run in test mode).
- For the Fleet plan: co-managers who answer requests and keep the calendar, and an organisation
  page for clubs and schools.
- Push notifications, maintenance reminders from flown hours, and in-app payments for rentals
  later.
