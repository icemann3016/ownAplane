# Plans, billing and AI credits

Renting and flying are free for pilots. Pilots who fly often can add **Pilot Plus**, owners choose
a plan for their aircraft, and everyone can buy credits for reading scans with AI. Prices are on the **Pricing** page (link at the bottom of every page) and
include VAT. Your plan, credits and payments are under **Account → Plan & billing**.

> **Test mode:** payments aren't live yet. You can subscribe, change and cancel exactly as later,
> but no card is asked for and nothing is charged; payments are marked "Test".

The Owner and Fleet plans include everything in Pilot Plus.

Safety and trust are the same on every plan: credential checks, eligibility, defects and
grounding, flight logs, reviews, messages and reports.

**During the beta** everyone has the Owner plan's features (Pilot Plus included) for free. Owners who list their
first aircraft during the beta are **founding owners**: they keep the Owner plan free for 12
months after the beta ends.

## Choosing, changing and cancelling

- **Choose a plan:** pick monthly or yearly (2 months free), press **Choose**, then pay at the
  checkout. The plan starts at once.
- **Upgrade** (a bigger plan, or monthly → yearly): starts now; you pay the new price less what
  is left of the current period.
- **Downgrade** (a smaller plan, or yearly → monthly): starts at your next renewal. Until then
  you keep what you paid for.
- **Cancel:** your plan runs to the end of the period you paid for, then you're on Free. You can
  change your mind before then (**Keep my current plan**).
- **A payment fails:** your plan continues for 7 days. Pay from Plan & billing to keep it.

## When your plan gets smaller

Nothing is ever deleted. A week before a smaller plan starts you get a notification if you have
more listed aircraft than it allows: unlist the ones you don't need. Otherwise, when it starts,
the most recently listed aircraft beyond the limit are unlisted (you can list them again after
upgrading), and featured labels beyond the plan's spots come off. Accepted bookings carry on.

## Pilot Plus

- **Currency** (Logbook → Currency): from your certified logbook entries, whether you may carry
  passengers (3 take-offs and landings in the last 90 days; at night, one of them at night), what
  is still missing to revalidate your SEP class rating by experience in the 12 months before it
  expires, and, with a LAPL only, the recency for it. You get a notification before your passenger
  currency runs out and when your SEP rating is due. It's a guide, not a legal check.
- **Logbook print and statistics:** the EASA print of your logbook (Logbook → Print) and your
  flying per month, per aircraft type, per function and your rentals (Logbook → Statistics). The
  logbook itself and its CSV export stay free on every plan.
- **20 saved searches** with alerts instead of 2. Searches you saved before stay.
- **Your bookings in your calendar** (Bookings): a private link for Google Calendar, Outlook or
  Apple Calendar with your bookings as pilot and as owner. Reset it if it got out.

## Featured aircraft

On Owner and Fleet, open **My aircraft → your aircraft** and press **Feature it**: the aircraft
comes first in search results, labelled "Featured", as long as it matches what pilots search for.

## AI reading credits

Filling in a licence, a medical or a paper logbook spread from a scan uses one credit. Every
account gets 5 free credits to try it. After that:

- Pilot Plus and Owner include 20 reads every month and Fleet 50 (not during the beta); they're used first.
- Credit packs (50 or 200) never expire.
- A read that fails, or finds nothing usable, gives its credit back.

You see what's left next to the scan buttons and under Plan & billing.
