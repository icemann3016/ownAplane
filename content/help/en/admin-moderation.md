# Reports and moderation (admins)

Admins keep the marketplace safe. Everything here is under **Admin** (account menu), with tabs
for the **Dashboard**, **Verifications**, **Reports**, **Members**, **Aircraft** and the
**Audit log**.

## Dashboard

Open reports, documents waiting for verification, and the last 30 days: members, pilots and
owners, listed aircraft, booking requests and how many were accepted, completed bookings and
reviews, cancellations (late ones, declined or expired), and sign-ups per week.

## Reports

Members report reviews, members, listings and messages. The queue shows the oldest open reports
first, with the reported content and the actions that fit:

- **Hide review** (and show again): only for abuse, personal data or spam, never for a merely
  negative review. Averages are updated.
- **Suspend member**: logs them out, stops them logging in, booking, messaging and reviewing, and
  unlists their aircraft. **Lift suspension** undoes it; they list their aircraft again
  themselves.
- **Unlist aircraft**: removes it from search; the owner can't list it again until you **Allow
  listing again**.
- **Mark resolved** or **Dismiss** when no action is needed. Acting on a report resolves it.

Every action asks for a reason (optional but recommended) and is written to the **audit log**,
which you can search by action, admin, target or reason. You can't suspend yourself.

**Members** (Admin → Members) lists everyone, with filters (pilots, owners, admins, suspended,
credentials to check, email not confirmed), search by name or email, and sorting. Each row shows
roles, whether the pilot is verified, flights, aircraft and when they were last active. Open a
member to see their account (email, sign-in methods, language, phone), pilot credentials status,
aircraft, bookings on both sides, open reports and the admin history of the account. All actions
are on that page:

- **Make admin** / **Remove admin rights**: admins can verify documents, handle reports and
  manage members, including other admins' rights, so only give this to people you trust. You
  can't remove your own admin rights, and a suspended member can't become an admin.
- **Suspend member** / **Lift suspension**, as above.

**Delete member** removes an account for good, for example when someone asks
by email or for a spam account: profile, credentials, documents, photos, and their aircraft with
its bookings. Bookings they made as a pilot, reviews and messages stay for the other side, shown
as a former member. It isn't possible while the member has open bookings (requested, accepted or
in progress): suspend them meanwhile. Admin accounts can't be deleted here; remove the admin role
first. Members can also delete their own account under **Account → Your data**.

## Practice flight

**Admin → Practice** lets you try the pilot's side of a rental on the live site: **Start a
practice flight** makes an accepted booking with you as pilot, starting now, on your own practice
aircraft (registration `ZZ-…`, a practice Cessna 172). You land on the booking: **Check out**,
add legs, fuel, oil and remarks, and check in like a pilot would. The practice aircraft is never
listed or searchable, and its practice owner gets no messages; the owner's confirmation isn't
part of it. Starting again replaces the previous practice flight; **Remove practice data**
deletes your practice aircraft with its bookings.
