# Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026. This policy explains what personal data ownAplane processes, why, and your rights under the GDPR.

## What we process and why

- **Account**: name, email, password (stored only as a hash), sign-in method (e.g. Google, Apple or Facebook), sessions (IP address, browser) and rate-limit records. *Why*: to run your account and keep it secure (contract; legitimate interest in security).
- **Profile** (public): display name, photo or avatar, home airfield, bio, roles, ratings and reviews. *Why*: so others know who they fly with (contract).
- **Settings** (private): language, units, contact phone. Your phone and email are shown only to the other side of an **accepted** booking (contract).
- **Pilot credentials**: licences, ratings, experience, date of birth (optional) and uploaded documents. **Medical certificates are special-category health data**: only you and our admins see them, and we process them only with your explicit consent, which you give by adding them (Art. 9(2)(a)). Public profiles show only verified licence types and ratings, never numbers, documents or medical data. Owners only get a yes/no "meets the requirements".
- **Aircraft**: listing details, photos (public), CofA/ARC/insurance documents (you and admins only).
- **Bookings and flight logs**: times, airfields, meter readings, fuel and oil, receipts, remarks, defects, amounts due, history of changes. Shared between the pilot and the owner of that booking (contract; legal claims).
- **Calendar sync**: an aircraft's owner can connect another booking system (for example a club's). That system receives the times the aircraft is booked and, only if the owner chooses, each booking's status, route and the pilot's name; never pilots' email addresses, phone numbers, credentials or flight logs. What it sends back (times, and titles if the owner chooses) is shown only to the owner (legitimate interest of the owner and pilots in avoiding double bookings).
- **Reviews, messages and reports**: reviews are public once published; messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation; admins see a message only when it has been reported to them. Reports are seen by the reporter and admins.
- **Notifications and emails** about your bookings, messages and documents (contract).
- **Admin audit log**: which admin took which decision, and when an admin opened a private document (legitimate interest in accountability).
- **Error reports and statistics**: if enabled, technical error reports and cookie-less, aggregated page statistics without personal profiles (legitimate interest in a working service).

## Who receives data

Only processors that help us run the service, under data processing agreements: hosting (Vercel, EU region Frankfurt), database and file storage (Supabase, EU), email delivery, and, if you choose them, Google, Apple or Facebook (Meta) for sign-in. Booking systems that an aircraft's owner connects receive only what is described under Calendar sync. The map on the search page loads tiles from OpenFreeMap, which receives your IP address. We never sell data.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your profile, settings, credentials, documents and aircraft. Records that the other side of a booking needs (bookings, flight logs, reviews, messages) stay without your name. Audit log entries are kept for [period]. Backups are overwritten within [period].

## Your rights

You can access and download your data (Account → Download my data), correct it, delete your account, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent for medical data at any time (delete it in Account → Pilot credentials) and complain to a supervisory authority; in Bulgaria that is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (https://www.cpdp.bg).

## Security

Access rules in the database decide who sees what (row-level security), private documents are served only through the app with access checks, passwords are hashed and all traffic is encrypted.
